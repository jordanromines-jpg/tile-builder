# R14 and tiles standing on a flat tile (proposed)

**Status:** proposed 9 Oct 2026, waiting for Jordan's go. Not part of the approved 4.0–4.2 plan.

## Why
4.2a fixed 61 of the 68 builds R14 found falling part-way through building. Seven are left on
`web/src/engine/r14-allow.json`:

| Build | What R14 sees | Real? |
|---|---|---|
| rained-indoors-camp | a tent (three triangles) on the field of flat squares tips about 21 mm while the next tent is held | no |
| planet-sparkle-crystal-cave | crystals (three triangles) on the cave floor tip, one folds right over | no |
| wrong-turn-mars-rover | a rock sample on the red squares slides about 20 mm | no |
| space-station-of-everything | the points' pyramids on the star-shaped deck tip about 16° | no |
| sierpinski-say-it-five-times | flat triangles of the pattern lift about 20 mm by the frame | no |
| triangle-truss-bridge | the two triangle sides and the road on top rack sideways like a parallelogram | yes |
| truck-spiral-ramp | the 6-high landing and the 8-high tower, one square across, sway over while they stand alone before the ramps tie them | yes |

Five are the physics model, not the builds. Measured on their own: three triangles leaning together stand on the table
(0 mm) and across four flat squares (0.8 mm), but **on one flat square they tip over (21 mm, 19°)**. A flat tile lying
on the table is joined to whatever stands on it by magnet hinges, and the solver lets that flat tile be levered up; real
tiles don't do that. Two quick changes were tried and put back (leaving out contacts between a flat tile and what
stands on it; joining three tiles on one hinge line as a chain): neither fixed it.

## What
1. Find why a flat tile on the table, joined to a pyramid standing on it, is levered up (the joints' anchors at the
   table, the hinge friction motor on a flat–standing pair, or the flat tile's slab above the table).
2. Change `physics/scene.ts` so a pyramid on one flat square stands, and add it to `calibration.test.ts` (C10), with
   C1–C9 still true.
3. Re-prove every build cold (`npm run check:physics --cold`, about 9 minutes on this Mac), fix anything that changes,
   take the five off the allow-list.
4. Triangle truss bridge: redesign so the sides can't rack (sides joined to the towers, or a braced end), and take it
   off.
5. Spiral ramp: two by two, the landing and the tower stand (tried), but need about 66 more squares than four Magna-Tiles
   100 have; redesign (a lower spiral, big squares for the tall tower, or the ramps built up round the tower as it
   rises). Then delete `r14-allow.json`, so R14 blocks every fall.

## Estimate
About 4 hours. Re-recording the truck runs afterwards (`npm run runs`) if the scene change touches them.
