# Tile Steps 2.0: the 10/10 sprint

Jordan rated the live 1.0 a 2/10: "the tiles don't look real and the 3D is terrible", and asked for the voice to be off.
This sprint keeps the engine, checker, 30 projects, storage, gate and routing, and replaces the look and feel. One
branch (`sprint-2`), one PR, deployed when green. No agents or workflows (D14).

## The changes

0. **Voice off** by default; only Hear again speaks. CI timing (#24): longer test and expect timeouts, plates wait
   for the network to settle.
1. **Real tiles** (`three/tile.ts`, `TileMesh.tsx`, `textures.ts`): thick glossy frames with rounded, bevelled
   corners; clear tinted faces with the moulded diamond texture as a bump map; chrome rivets.
2. **Light and table** (`three/Stage.tsx`): a room environment made on the device, neutral tone mapping, key and rim
   lights, a wooden table drawn on a canvas, soft contact shadows.
3. **Camera** (`three/camera.ts`, `Viewer.tsx`): a three-quarter view framed to the model's bounding sphere; it frames
   what is built so far and pulls back as the build grows; each step leans toward the new tiles; a full-screen stage
   frames the model in the part the panels leave clear.
4. **Magnetic building** (`three/Model.tsx`, `anim.ts`): a pulsing ghost where the tiles go, then each glides in on an
   arc and settles with a small snap; earlier tiles quieten; the placed ones glow softly.
5. **Toy box** art direction: warm paper, wood, a sunny burnt-orange accent (`#BF5409`, `#FF9B45` dark), every
   contrast pair passing; new app icon.
6. **Library as a toy shelf**: a wordmark, the age picker beside it, big cards with 3D pictures standing on wooden
   planks, a compact "add your tiles" banner.
7. **Build screen**: the 3D stage fills the screen; floating round buttons; a step strip with the tiles, the line,
   Back and a 112 px Next.
8. **3D pictures** (`pictures.ts`, `three/pictures.ts`, `scripts/pictures.mjs`): tile chips and project cards are
   drawn ahead of time from the stage's own 3D code and shipped as WebP (1.5 MB, cached offline); SVG is the fallback.
9. **The end**: the view circles the model once under a shower of little tiles, then a photo card and Back to the
   shelf.
10. **Polish**: one press feel, soft layered shadows, iOS status bar over the app, safe areas.

## Build log

- Voice off and CI timing first; then the 3D (iterated on screenshots of the castle at steps 1, 14 and 25); tokens;
  pictures; screens; tests and plates.
- Found on the way: an instant camera move (reduced motion, a jump with the step dots) never moved the camera; a
  long card title squeezed the stars; making the shared light changed the picture renderer's viewport on first use;
  the end's timer counted from mounting, so a slow first draw ate the celebration.
- Rebuilt, not patched: the first version drew the 3D pictures on the iPad at run time (an offscreen renderer, a
  queue, idle callbacks). In the browser tests' software renderer that held the page up for seconds, and each fix
  added another workaround. It was thrown out for pictures drawn once ahead of time. Likewise the build-mode plates
  stopped trying to hide a full-screen canvas: they show the whole screen, which is still and drawn the same each time
  with motion reduced.
- A software renderer gets a lighter stage (`gpu.ts`); the frame-rate test measures once the first frames (shader
  compiles) are drawn; the step line no longer scrolls inside the strip (axe).
- **Review** (five read-only reviewers, each finding checked by a skeptic; 31 confirmed, all fixed): turning the
  model swung a part-built model out of view (the aim now turns with it); the end's circle froze part-way when the
  shower ended or was tapped (it eases back, and finishes first); the aim jumped on each step for 6–10 (the camera rig
  owns the orbit target and eases from where it is); reduced motion still animated quarter turns; frames already in
  place never faded in again (an opaque shader); the stage ignored a light/dark switch; the glow kept the stage drawing
  for as long as a step was open, behind the rest screen too (it pulses 2.5 s, then holds still); the 9–10 model spun
  forever on the end screen; shared geometry and textures outlived the stage; clear faces were drawn twice a frame;
  the orange glow tinted blue tiles purple (tiles glow in their own colour); iPads that had 1.0 kept the voice on (a
  one-time switch to off); tap-to-say chips did nothing with the voice off (pictures, not buttons, until it is on);
  the end had no Hear again; kid targets 12 px apart (24 now); the resume badge's small type; the wood's raw colours
  (from the `ground` token now); a Python token test still on the old palette; reloads in the browser tests raced the
  step save; the castle plates were taken behind its "need more tiles" note; stale docs and a committed build cache.
