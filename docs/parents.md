# Tile Steps: for grown-ups

Tile Steps helps a child build with magnet tiles, one step at a time, from a 3D picture. It is free, has no
ads or purchases, and keeps everything on your iPad.

## Put it on the iPad (two minutes)

1. Open **https://jordanromines-jpg.github.io/tile-builder/** in Safari on the iPad.
2. Tap **Share** at the top of Safari, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Tile Steps from the Home Screen from now on.

Why the Home Screen: there, iPadOS keeps the app's data, and the app works with Wi-Fi off. In a Safari tab, Safari may
clear it after a week or so unused.

## Enter your tiles (three minutes)

1. Tap the small lock at the top right. Hold it for three seconds, then answer the sum. This keeps the grown-ups side
   away from small hands.
2. Tap **Tiles**, then **Start from a set** and pick yours (Magna-Tiles, PicassoTiles or Connetix).
3. Use − and + for tiles that are lost or extra. Colours are optional.
4. Lay a tall triangle next to two stacked squares and tap the picture that matches.

The shelves then show which projects you can build now, which need a swap, and which need a few more tiles. Nothing
ever says "buy".

## Building together

Stand the iPad up beside the tiles. Your child picks an age picture (3–5, 6–8 or 9–10) and a project. Tap **Hear
again** (the speaker) to have a step read aloud, or turn reading on for every step in Settings. For the youngest, some steps ask you to hold a wall.

## Backups

On the grown-ups side, **Save a backup** makes one small file (your tiles, settings and builds in progress). Keep it in
Files. **Restore from a backup** puts it back, on this iPad or a new one.

## What it never does

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no purchases, no camera, no streaks, timers or scores. Nothing is sent anywhere: the
backup file is the only way anything leaves the iPad, and only when you save it.

To keep a child inside the app, turn on **Guided Access** in the iPad's Settings, under Accessibility.
